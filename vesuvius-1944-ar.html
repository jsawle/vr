What plays in AR

The AR model plays the eruption as a loop of about 66 seconds and then starts again. A sign in front of the model shows the date and what is happening.

    Heights are 1.5 times real so the slopes are easier to see. Lava thickness and the ash cloud are drawn to be seen, not to scale.

    How to open it on a table
    1. Wait for “AR model ready”, then tap View in AR.
    2. Point the camera at a table or the floor and move it slowly until the model appears.
    3. Pinch to make it bigger or smaller, drag to move it, and twist with two fingers to turn it.
    4. Walk around it or move the device closer to look at the towns and the lava.
    5. The Object tab at the top shows the model without the camera, which works on a small desk.

    Apple's AR viewer has no play controls of its own: the model plays from the start each time you open it. Sound isn't included yet.

    About and versions

    This is a lab version for testing, made from the VR version. It works out the lava flows on your device, builds a 3D model file and hands it to Apple's AR viewer. The VR version stays as it is.
